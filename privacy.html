Privacy

Orbital Puck does not require a game account.

Updated: October 4, 2026

The Apple Vision Pro game

Version 1.0 is a self-contained game with no advertising, analytics SDK, external account or developer-operated game server. The app’s code does not send scores or hand positions to a developer service.

Hand tracking

With your permission, the app uses ARKit to read your right-hand position and control the paddle during play. Positions are processed in memory on the device; the game does not save them to files or send them to a server. You can decline or withdraw permission in Settings and use pinch controls.

This website

These static pages do not include analytics scripts or set tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical information needed to operate it under its privacy statement.

Support form

If you use the form, your name, email and message are sent to Web3Forms for delivery to Rodolphe Duperray, the publisher of Orbital Puck, to handle your request. Web3Forms may also process technical information for security and abuse prevention. Its processing, service providers and retention periods are described in its privacy policy. Do not send sensitive information.

Questions and requests

To ask for information, correction or deletion of a support message, use the support form. Processing by Apple, including purchases and system diagnostics, is governed by Apple’s own settings and policies.
