Getting started

Enter and start

Select Enter the café, then Start match on the scoreboard. The first player to seven points wins.

Guide the paddle

Allow hand tracking, then move your right hand with your palm flat. Block gently or strike quickly to speed up the puck. Use Recenter or Recenter hand to adjust the reference point.

Use pinch controls

In Station settings, select Pinch. Look at the turquoise paddle, pinch and move it. This mode is available when hand permission is denied and in the Simulator.

Change destinations

Open the airlock behind you or select Open airlock · Destinations. Choose one of the nine cards, then start a new match. Opening the airlock pauses the match; close it to resume.

Common questions

Hand out of view?

Keep your right hand visible in front of you for a moment to resume tracking. At the table edge, bring your hand back into play. If tracking stays unavailable, use pinch controls.

How do I adjust comfort and sound?

Settings include table height, pause, recentering and a music and sound effects switch. Take a break if you feel uncomfortable.

How do I change language?

Select French or English in the app’s language preference, then relaunch Orbital Puck. Language follows visionOS system settings.

Do I need an account?

No game account is required. The game is single-player; the support form is separate from the app.
